About the maison

Devoted to the details that hold a bag together.

Buckled is an independent website about buckle bags and the craft behind them.

We love bags that are built to last and to be repaired: honest leather, solid hardware, careful stitching. Our guides explain what makes a good buckle bag and how to keep one beautiful for years.

We do not sell bags, use affiliate links or accept payment for mentions. Every guide is reviewed by a second editor and dated.
